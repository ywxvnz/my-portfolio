My Sugar Journal: A Blood Sugar Logging System

PHP · CodeIgniter 3 · MySQL · Bootstrap 5 · JavaScript · Chart.js · Dompdf

My Sugar Journal is a web-based blood sugar logging system that helps users record, monitor, and better understand their blood sugar levels. It was my first concept before developing Glucomate for our thesis.

View on GitHub ↗

The system digitizes the traditional notebook-based method of recording blood sugar readings. Users can create an account, add, view, edit, and delete blood sugar logs, attach notes, filter records, visualize glucose trends through charts, and export records as PDF files. Each reading is categorized based on its level—such as low, normal, or high—to make monitoring easier.

Project Information

July 31, 2025

An individually developed OJT project that involved CRUD as our activity. I handled the planning, UI design, database design, CRUD functionality, user authentication, blood sugar level categorization, chart visualization, filtering, and PDF export features. It was presented to the MIS Team of the ICT Department at St. Dominic College of Asia (SDCA).
